## What works

Played on the production server at `localhost:3000`, with no model key. Sample mode is labeled on the page.

- **The Gatekeeper's Ledger.** `O'Brien` stops the scribe. The spell marks `Brien`, the part that fell out of the quote, and the stage says the scribe stopped. `' OR '1'='1` opens the vault: the door is on the stage, two ledger lines come back, and the words that left the quotes are marked. Ada's reading can differ. The judge tests reject a reply that repeats the hidden law.
- **Peppered Forest.** Soot darkens the bark at once and the moths stay pale, with the hunting bird in front of them. Turning the birds off lifts the hunter before the next generation. One generation on sooty bark brings dark wings with the new landing. The stage says the moths are not the colour they were. A run with the birds away does not announce a colour change from drift.
- **Rumour Town.** The bank stands on the horizon and the crowd stands on the cobbles. Eight rumours and no insurance board the door and the stage says the bank has closed its doors. The same rumour with deposit insurance leaves the bank open (nobody withdraws). Ada, on the sample lane, can still show a collapse.
- **The Firebreak.** Turning the strip on plants a lane of bare earth before **Let it burn**. A dense, windy planting burns to that path and stops: ash, the lane, then living trees. The stage says the fire stopped.
- **Newton's Cannon.** A slow shot meets the ground and stays on the rim. A fast shot has time to leave. The slider draws the next arc before the shot.
