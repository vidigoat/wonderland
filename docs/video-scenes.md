# Demo video: scene by scene (target 2:05)

Each take is one recording, made in one Chrome window with the face cam on the side. Say the line, do the action, then stop.

## Before you record

1. Open a new Chrome window at 1440×900 with three tabs:
   - **Tab 1:** https://wonderland-omega-fawn.vercel.app
   - **Tab 2:** https://wonderland-omega-fawn.vercel.app/w/peppered-forest
   - **Tab 3:** https://wonderland-omega-fawn.vercel.app/teach/peppered-forest
2. Click through each tab once so the paintings load. Then reload Tabs 1 and 2 so they start fresh.
3. Allow the microphone when Chrome asks. Scene 7 needs it.
4. **Record take C first.** A build takes 2–5 minutes. Keep recording through the wait, and I'll cut it down.

## Answers, so the takes go smoothly (keep them off camera)

- **Scene 6:** the middle option ("Dark moths become common…").
- **Take D ends after two runs,** which is exactly what take E needs before **I think I see the rule** will work. Don't reload the page between D and E.
- **If the microphone gives you trouble in take E,** type the sentence instead and click **Offer this sentence**.
- **Scene 7 sentence:** "Birds eat the moths that stand out, so the ones that match the bark survive and pass on their colour."
- **Scene 7, the new situation:** the middle option ("White hares become common…").

## If something goes wrong

- **If the build offers an existing world instead of building a new one,** use a different question: **Why does mould spread across a slice of bread?**
- **If a world fails a check,** keep recording. A repair followed by "Watched again" makes an even better scene 5.
