# Demo video — 3 minutes

A suggested shot list for the recording. Everything listed here is in the app as built; record on the production build (`pnpm build && pnpm start`) in a fresh 1440×900 window. Build one new world the night before (it takes 2–5 minutes) so you can show its build in full and cut it down.

| Time | Beat | What's on screen | Say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Hook** | Landing hero: *"Don't ask AI for the answer. Ask it for a world."* over the drifting wall of paintings. | "People who learn with an AI assistant score 17 points lower on understanding. So I built the opposite." |
| 0:12 | **Try it, in 30 seconds** | Scroll to *Try it*. Pick *"The bank collapses."* Watch Rumour Town fall. *Find what saves it* → hide the queue → still falls → guarantee deposits → *"You found it."* | "One false rumour, a healthy bank. I predict, I watch, then I go looking for what saves it. Nobody tells me. That's a real law of economics, and I found it." |
| 0:45 | **Ask anything** | Type a fresh question, *Build my world*. Show the steps lighting up and the law as `███`; cut to *"All 5 passed, on every seed"* and the painting step. | "Any question becomes a world. Muse Spark Open finds the law and writes the world as code, the code runs against the science on every seed, and Muse Image and SAM 3.1 paint it. The law never reaches my browser." |
| 1:15 | **Inside a world** | Enter it (or Peppered Forest). The mission card: predict, experiment, say it, prove it. Make a prediction, move a control, run it. | "The world tells me what to do, not what's true." |
| 1:40 | **The race** | Ada's card: her reasoning and the *You vs Ada* readings. | "Ada is an AI scientist on Meta's open weights, racing me on the same world." |
| 1:55 | **Say it** | *I can say it* → hold to speak (Muse Voice Transcribe) or type → the judge's reply. | "I say the law in my own words. The judge tells me how close I am, and it is never allowed to say the answer." |
| 2:15 | **Prove it** | The new situation (e.g. snowy tundra for Peppered Forest) → *Offer the proof* → the card you keep. | "Then a situation I've never seen. If I understood it, I can predict it." |
| 2:35 | **Worlds the models made** | Scroll the world grid: fireflies, predator and prey, traffic, heat, dunes, all painted. | "Five worlds I built by hand. The rest were written, verified and painted by Meta's models on their own." |
| 2:50 | **Close** | The hero again, or the logo. | "The AI does the building. You do the thinking. Wonderland." |

## Models on screen
- **Muse Spark Open** (Meta open weights, Baseten): scientist, designer, judge, Ada, the 40 citizens.
- **Muse Image 1.0 + SAM 3.1**: every painting and every cut-out creature.
- **Muse Voice Transcribe**: spoken hypotheses.
